The library spans machinery, crops, harvest, robotics, field sensing and food systems. The 6-second silent MP4s and 3-second GIF excerpts are derivatives for web layout experiments.

Wikimedia Commons items carry the exact file-page license and attribution. For Pexels items, the Pexels License permits free personal and commercial use without attribution; credit is provided as a courtesy. Do not resell unmodified stock media or reuse it in a way that implies a depicted person/brand endorses the project. Review the source page before public release.
